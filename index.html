Everyday Acupressure

Acupressure for the things that come up in a normal week: headaches, a stiff neck, stress, poor sleep, an upset stomach. Pick what you're feeling, then tap a point to zoom in.

Pregnant or think you might be? Don't use acupressure before consulting your OB-GYN or midwife. Several points here are traditionally avoided in pregnancy.

Points marked ✕ are traditionally avoided in pregnancy. If you're trying to conceive, treat the second half of your cycle as "could be".

Medical disclaimer

This guide is for general information and self-care only. It is not medical advice and does not replace a diagnosis or treatment from a qualified clinician. Do not use acupressure on yourself if you are pregnant or think you might be, unless your OB-GYN or midwife has approved it. Check with a clinician first if you have a bleeding or clotting disorder, take blood thinners, have a pacemaker or heart condition, diabetes with nerve damage in the feet, or osteoporosis. Don't press on broken skin, bruises, varicose veins, or recent injuries. Stop if anything feels sharp, numb, or wrong. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
