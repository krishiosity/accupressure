Medical disclaimer

This guide is for general information and self-care only. It is not medical advice and does not replace a diagnosis or treatment from a qualified clinician. Do not use acupressure on yourself if you are pregnant or think you might be, unless your OB-GYN or midwife has approved it. Check with a clinician first if you have a bleeding or clotting disorder, take blood thinners, have a pacemaker or heart condition, or have unexplained pelvic pain or bleeding. Stop if anything feels sharp, numb, or wrong. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
